Privacy Policy

Effective from October 11, 2026

In short

  • SudokuScan collects no personal data, requires no registration and contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only to scan a printed sudoku. The photo is processed on the phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • The app opens a photo from your library only when you choose it yourself.
  • The app saves to Photos only an image that you share and save yourself.

Digit recognition

  • Digits are recognized on the device (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android) without an internet connection.

Data storage

  • Saved sudoku, games in progress, statistics and settings are stored only on this device.
  • You can delete everything in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing

  • Sharing happens only at your request through the phone’s system share sheet; the app itself sends nothing.

Children

  • The app is suitable for all ages and processes no data about children.
